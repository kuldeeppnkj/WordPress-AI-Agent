An AI-powered shopping and customer-support assistant for E-commerce and
WooCommerce stores. Help customers discover products, find best sellers
and favourites, check inventory, use coupons,
understand payment methods, get answers from FAQs, and connect with live
chat from one intelligent chatbot.

== Description ==

E-commerce AI Agent brings an intelligent conversational assistant
directly to your E-commerce website.

Instead of making customers browse multiple pages, search through large
product catalogs, or look for answers in separate support sections, the
AI Agent can provide a conversational shopping and support experience.

Designed for WooCommerce-powered stores, the plugin can help customers
discover products, understand store information, find deals, and get
support through a single chatbot interface.

The AI Agent can be used as a:

-   AI shopping assistant
-   Product catalog search assistant
-   Customer support chatbot
-   FAQ assistant
-   Product discovery tool
-   Store information assistant
-   Coupon and promotion assistant
-   Inventory availability assistant
-   Gift card information assistant
-   Payment information assistant
-   Live-chat entry point

== Key Features ==

= AI Shopping Assistant =

Allow customers to interact with your store using natural language
instead of relying only on traditional search and navigation.

Examples:

-   “Show me wireless headphones under $100.”
-   “I need a gift for a photographer.”
-   “Which products are popular?”
-   “What do you recommend for a birthday gift?”
-   “Find me a red shirt in medium.”
-   “Do you have anything similar to this product?”

= Product Catalog Search =

Help customers search and discover products conversationally.

The assistant can help with:

-   Product names
-   Product categories
-   Product descriptions
-   Product attributes
-   Product variations
-   Prices
-   Product availability
-   Product recommendations
-   Related products
-   Product discovery based on customer requirements

= Best-Selling Products =

Help customers discover products that are selling well in the store.

Customers can ask questions such as:

-   “What are your best sellers?”
-   “Show me your most popular products.”
-   “What products do customers buy the most?”
-   “Recommend a popular product.”

= Favourite and Popular Products =

Help customers discover popular, highly viewed, frequently purchased, or
otherwise highlighted products, depending on the store’s available
product data and configuration.

The AI Agent can guide customers toward products that match their
interests and shopping intent.

= Gift Cards =

Make it easier for customers to find information about gift cards.

The chatbot can help answer questions such as:

-   “Do you sell gift cards?”
-   “What gift card options are available?”
-   “How can I use a gift card?”
-   “Can I buy a gift card as a present?”

Gift-card capabilities depend on the gift-card functionality available
in the store.

= Coupons and Discounts =

Help customers understand available promotions and coupon-related
information.

Customers can ask:

-   “Do you have any coupons?”
-   “How do I use a coupon?”
-   “Is there a discount available?”
-   “Why isn’t my coupon working?”

The plugin can provide configured coupon and promotion information while
respecting the store’s existing WooCommerce rules and restrictions.

= Product Inventory and Availability =

Help customers find out whether products or variations are available.

Example questions:

-   “Is this product in stock?”
-   “Do you have this in size large?”
-   “Is the blue version available?”
-   “When will this item be available?”

Inventory responses depend on the product and stock information
available to the plugin.

= Payment Methods =

Answer common questions about the store’s supported payment methods.

Customers can ask:

-   “What payment methods do you accept?”
-   “Can I pay by card?”
-   “Do you accept PayPal?”
-   “What payment options are available at checkout?”

The AI Agent can surface payment information configured or documented
for the store.

= FAQs and Knowledge Base =

Turn your existing frequently asked questions and store information into
a conversational support experience.

Useful topics include:

-   Shipping
-   Returns
-   Refunds
-   Exchanges
-   Order information
-   Delivery times
-   Payment methods
-   Account questions
-   Store policies
-   Product questions
-   Gift cards
-   Coupons
-   General store information

= Live Chat =

Give customers a conversational entry point to customer support.

When the AI Agent cannot fully resolve a customer request, stores can
provide a path to live human support, depending on the live-chat/support
solution configured for the website.

= Product Recommendations =

Help customers find products based on natural-language requirements.

For example:

-   Budget
-   Product type
-   Category
-   Use case
-   Size
-   Color
-   Features
-   Preferences
-   Gift recipient
-   Shopping intent

= Conversational Product Discovery =

Customers do not need to know the exact product name.

They can describe what they need and the AI Agent can help narrow down
suitable products.

Example:

“I need a lightweight laptop for travel under $800.”

= Store Information Assistant =

The chatbot can be configured to answer common questions about your
store, policies, services, and customer experience.

= WooCommerce Integration =

Built with WooCommerce stores in mind, the plugin can work with

WooCommerce is required for WooCommerce-specific product, inventory, and
shopping features.

== Installation ==

1.  Download the E-commerce AI Agent plugin.
2.  In your E-commerce dashboard, go to Plugins > Add New Plugin.
3.  Upload the plugin ZIP file.
4.  Install and activate the plugin.
5.  If you are running an online store, install and configure
    WooCommerce.
6.  Open the E-commerce AI Agent settings.
7.  Configure the AI provider, chatbot settings, store data, FAQs, and
    other available options.
8.  Save your settings.
9.  Open your website and test the chatbot.

== Configuration ==

After activation, configure the plugin according to your store’s

Typical configuration areas may include:

-   AI provider/API configuration
-   Chatbot appearance
-   Welcome message
-   Store information
-   Product data
-   FAQs and knowledge
-   Support information
-   Coupon and promotion information
-   Gift-card information
-   Payment-method information
-   Live-chat/support settings
-   AI response behavior

Available settings depend on the version and integrations installed on

== Requirements ==

-   E-commerce 6.0 or later
-   PHP 7.4 or later
-   WooCommerce for WooCommerce-specific functionality
-   An AI provider/API configured for AI-powered features
-   A properly configured E-commerce website

For production websites, use a currently supported version of E-commerce,
PHP, WooCommerce, and your selected AI provider.

== Privacy and Security ==

The plugin should be configured carefully when handling customer
conversations and store data.

Depending on the configuration and AI provider used, chatbot messages
may be processed by third-party AI services.

Before deploying the plugin on a production website:

-   Review your AI provider’s privacy policy and terms.
-   Do not expose secret API keys in client-side code.
-   Keep API credentials in secure server-side configuration.
-   Avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive customer information
    to external services.
-   Review your website’s privacy policy and applicable data-protection
    requirements.
-   Keep E-commerce, WooCommerce, the plugin, and other dependencies
    updated.

The plugin should not be treated as a replacement for your store’s
existing authentication, authorization, payment security, or privacy
controls.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does the plugin work with WooCommerce? =

Yes. The plugin is designed to provide WooCommerce-focused shopping and
support features. WooCommerce is required for WooCommerce product and
store functionality.

= Can customers search products using normal language? =

Yes. The AI Agent is designed to understand natural-language shopping
requests and help customers discover relevant products.

= Can the chatbot show products? =

The AI Agent can use available product information to help customers
discover and recommend products. The exact presentation depends on the
plugin version and configuration.

= Can it answer questions about inventory? =

Yes, where the required WooCommerce product and stock information is
available to the plugin.

= Can it answer questions about coupons? =

It can provide configured or available coupon and promotion information.
Coupon validation and application remain subject to WooCommerce and the
store’s configured rules.

= Can it provide gift-card information? =

Yes, where gift-card functionality or store information is available and
configured.

= Can it answer payment questions? =

Yes. The assistant can explain configured or documented payment methods
and related store information.

= Can customers talk to a human? =

The plugin can provide a live-chat or human-support handoff where a
compatible support/live-chat solution is configured.

= Does the AI Agent replace customer service staff? =

No. It is designed to assist customers and automate common questions.
Stores should provide a human support path for complex, sensitive, or
unresolved requests.

= Does the plugin require an AI API? =

AI-powered functionality requires a supported AI provider or AI service
configured for the plugin.

= Is the plugin free? =

Check the plugin’s current distribution and licensing information for
the applicable version.
